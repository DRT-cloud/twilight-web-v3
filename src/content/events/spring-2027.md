Held at The Burial Mound Shooting Center, Pawnee, Oklahoma.

- **Friday, May 21** — NV 2Gun / NV PCC — Night Vision only.
- **Saturday, May 22** — 2Gun / PCC — White Light.
- Gates open: 5:00 PM each night.
- Check-in: 7:00–8:00 PM each night.
- Shooters meeting: 9:00 PM each night.
- First release: 10:00 PM each night.
- Entry fee: $100 per division; $50 second division (same competitor, max 2).
- Includes: Twilight Biathlon t-shirt for every registered competitor.
- Awards: Top 3 in each division; ceremony held Saturday night after all scoring is complete.
- Payment: PayPal. Details sent by email after PractiScore registration. Registration is not complete until PayPal payment is received.
